INSIDE THE LAB

Hi, I’m Ghita.

My background sits between business, data and technology. I studied management and marketing, then somehow ended up working with requirements, data exchanges, APIs, Python, reporting and the systems behind them.

Most of my day-to-day work starts the same way: someone has a problem, several systems or people are involved, and the obvious answer usually isn’t the whole answer. So I ask questions, pull the workflow apart, follow the data and work with the people who actually use or build the thing. Ghita Labs is where I do that independently.
